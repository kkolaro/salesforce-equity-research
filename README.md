# Salesforce Equity Research

Fundamental research on Salesforce (NYSE: CRM) by Sofia Kolaro, examining valuation, business performance, and the opportunities and risks of agentic AI.

The report includes financial forecasts, DCF and reverse DCF valuation, peer comparisons, and scenario analysis. It concludes with a BUY recommendation and a $223.23 target price, based on the August 5, 2026 reference price of $192.98.
